# Data Policy

1. Prefer official government and intergovernmental sources.
2. Preserve the original source URL and publisher.
3. Do not transform missing observations into estimated national scores.
4. Do not compare figures with incompatible definitions as if they were the same metric.
5. Keep historical observations rather than overwriting them.
6. Automated parsing may propose updates; failed or ambiguous parsing keeps the last verified value.
7. Add new source domains explicitly to `data/sources.json`.
8. Do not let arbitrary web pages enter the training corpus automatically.
9. Model promotion must remain gated by evaluation and rollback capability.
